02Production & technology

Every piece, frozen on its own.

Individual Quick Freezing locks each piece separately — before large ice crystals can damage the cell walls. Taste, texture and nutrition stay as they were on harvest day, and there is never a solid block in the bag.

Conventional block freezingOne solid block

Pieces fuse together and crystals rupture cells — flavour and nutrients drain on thawing.

Frosty IQFFree-flowing pieces

Each piece frozen individually — portion exactly what's needed, keep the rest, zero waste.
